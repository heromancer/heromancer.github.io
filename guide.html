1. Heroes — the roster

Every hero is a star-rated NFT (★1–★5) in your wallet: playable in your party. Hero NFTs are access passes as well as fighters, and they carry no promised monetary value — their purpose is access and play, nothing else. A hero's Power = ATK × 2 + DEF + HP ÷ 10. Heroes gain levels (1–50) from battle EXP — each level adds +4% base stats — and duplicates can be fused: burn 2 duplicates of the same star to raise one hero by +1★ (max ★5). Burned heroes are destroyed forever.

Party & account levels. Your party holds up to 5 heroes: slots 1–3 from account level 1, the 4th at level 10, the 5th at level 20. Battles earn EXP (1 EXP per Loot); account level also unlocks gear crafting (Lv5) and fusion (Lv15).

2. Battle — zones & loot

Your party auto-battles in one zone at a time, 24/7 — even while you're offline (earnings accumulate up to the 12-hour cap). Loot per hour = 10 × zone multiplier × (Party Power ÷ 160), so a party at Power 160 — one Genesis Warden, for example — earns the starter 10 Loot/hr, and every point of Power you add pays directly. Each zone has a Power gate: the minimum Party Power to enter.

5. Earning — turning Loot into $HMANCER

Playing pays in four steps. (1) Battle: your party earns Loot automatically, 24/7, at the zone formula above — offline earnings accumulate up to the 12-hour cap. (2) Claim: check in and collect the Loot. (3) Sell: exchange Loot for $HMANCER at the live rate, which floats with the Rewards Pool — the fuller the pool, the better the rate; rewards never exceed what the pool holds. (4) Spend or trade: put $HMANCER back into summons and upgrades (90% of that spend recycles to the pool, 10% is burned), or trade it freely once the token lists.
