1. Heroes — the roster

Every hero is a star-rated NFT (★1–★5) in your wallet: playable in your party and tradable with other players. A hero's Power = ATK × 2 + DEF + HP ÷ 10. Heroes gain levels (1–50) from battle EXP — each level adds +4% base stats — and duplicates can be fused: burn 2 duplicates of the same star to raise one hero by +1★ (max ★5). Burned heroes are destroyed forever.

The Genesis Hero — a special ★★★ Knight (HP 500 · ATK 38 · DEF 24 · Power 160) minted only by registration. It is your account key and first fighter, is never in the summon pool, and is capped at 5,000 forever. When the 5,000 are gone, registration continues at $1 for game access only — no NFT; access-only players summon their first heroes with $HMANCER once summons open.

Party & account levels. Your party holds up to 5 heroes: slots 1–3 from account level 1, the 4th at level 10, the 5th at level 20. Battles earn EXP (1 EXP per Loot); account level also unlocks gear crafting (Lv5) and fusion (Lv15).

2. Battle — zones & loot

Your party auto-battles in one zone at a time, 24/7 — even while you're offline (earnings accumulate up to the 12-hour cap). Loot per hour = 10 × zone multiplier × (Party Power ÷ 160), so a fresh Genesis Hero earns the starter 10 Loot/hr, and every point of Power you add pays directly. Each zone has a Power gate: the minimum Party Power to enter.

4. Gear & skills

Gear — three slots per hero: Weapon, Armor, Trinket. Gear is an in-game item (heroes are the NFTs). It drops from battle zones and can be crafted: burn 3 pieces of one rarity to forge 1 of the next (account Lv5+).
