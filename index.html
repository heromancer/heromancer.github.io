Questions, answered

When does Heromancer launch?

Registration (the Genesis Hero NFT mint) is open now. The $HMANCER token listing and the battle opening come next: Season 1 opens December 1, 2026.

How do I register?

On the register page: send exactly 0.0145 SOL (≈$1) from a wallet you control to the address shown there. Our system detects the payment and mints your Genesis Hero NFT to that same wallet, usually within about 10 minutes. Never pay from an exchange — the NFT would go to the exchange's wallet.

What do I get for the $1?

One Hero NFT — a ★★★ Genesis hero: your account key and your first fighter in one. It is minted to your wallet when the $1 lands — minting costs a small fraction of the $1. Pay once, keep the hero — there is no subscription.

How do I earn $HMANCER?

Your heroes battle automatically. Claim their Loot and sell it in the in-game Market for $HMANCER. The exchange rate floats with the Rewards Pool, so payouts stay within what the pool holds.

How do leaderboard seasons work?

Seasons are monthly. The leaderboard tracks your season score (loot earned + power gained), resets on the 1st of every month, and the finished season's Top 500 split 10,000 USDT, paid in SOL — rank 1 takes $1,000. Your heroes, gear and account level never reset, only the race does. Prizes come from the Rewards Pool; if the pool holds less than the full prize, shares scale down proportionally.

Can I sell my heroes?

Yes — every hero is a transferable NFT in your wallet. Sell or trade heroes player-to-player on any Solana NFT marketplace: a duplicate can fund your next summon, and a ★5 you pull can be sold to another player. The Genesis hero also carries the account access.

Do I have to play every day?

No. Offline loot piles up for up to 12 hours, so checking in once or twice a day is enough. Battles are calculated server-side, so changing your device clock does nothing.

Is $HMANCER an investment?

No. It is a game token: its price is set by traders and can go to zero. Registration buys a game access NFT, not an investment, and there is no guarantee of any price, earnings, or return.
