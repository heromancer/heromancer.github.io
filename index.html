An economy that loops

Player rewards are paid from a Rewards Pool — funded by 50% of every registration plus trading fees from $HMANCER trading, from the trading launch on December 15, 2026. The Pre-Registration Box token prizes (2,480,000 $HMANCER) are paid from the game-rewards allocation, straight from the treasury. The Loot→$HMANCER rate floats with the pool, so rewards never exceed what the pool holds. Upgrades recycle 90% back to the pool and burn 10% forever: the more people play, the scarcer $HMANCER gets. Battles are calculated server-side — the server is the referee, clock tricks do nothing.

Questions, answered

When does Heromancer launch?

Pre-registration is open now — every registration opens a Pre-Registration Box on the spot. $HMANCER trading opens December 15, 2026, the Genesis Warden claim window opens December 29, 2026, and the battle opens with Season 1 on January 1, 2027.

How do I register?

On the register page: send exactly 0.0145 SOL (≈$1) from a wallet you control to the address shown there. Our system detects the payment and opens your Pre-Registration Box automatically: a $HMANCER prize is transferred straight to that same wallet, and if your box holds the Genesis Warden NFT, your entitlement is recorded to that wallet (the NFT itself is minted in the claim window opening December 29, 2026). Never pay from an exchange — the prizes would go to the exchange's wallet. Once the 10,000 boxes sell out, the same payment registers game access only — no box.

What do I get for the $1?

Game entry plus one Pre-Registration Box (while the 10,000 last) that opens the moment your payment lands: 9,980 boxes hold $HMANCER (100–1,000, paid straight to your wallet) and 20 hold the Genesis Warden ★★★ NFT (entitlement recorded instantly; the NFT is minted in the claim window opening December 29, 2026). Whatever the box holds, every registered account also receives a free account-bound ★1 starter hero (Bram the Squire) at launch, so everyone plays from day one. After the boxes sell out, the $1 buys game access only — no box. Hero NFTs carry no promised monetary value — the $1 buys your entry and your box. Either way: pay once, no subscription.

How do I earn $HMANCER?

Your heroes battle automatically. Claim their Loot and sell it in the in-game Market for $HMANCER. The exchange rate floats with the Rewards Pool, so payouts stay within what the pool holds.

How do leaderboard seasons work?

Seasons are monthly. The leaderboard tracks your season score (loot earned + power gained), resets on the 1st of every month, and the finished season's Top 500 split 10,000 USDT, paid in SOL — rank 1 takes $1,000. Your heroes, gear and account level never reset, only the race does. Prizes come from the Rewards Pool; if the pool holds less than the full prize, shares scale down proportionally.

What is the NFT actually for?

Hero NFTs are playable characters and access passes that live in your wallet — the Genesis Warden and every hero you summon. The game reads your party from your wallet and lets you play them. They carry no promised monetary value: we do not promise they can be sold for anything. Heroes are transferable, so a hero — and the access that follows it — moves with the NFT if you pass it to someone else, but its purpose is playing the game, not making money. And you never need one just to start: your free account-bound ★1 starter hero arrives at launch either way.

Do I have to play every day?

No. Offline loot piles up for up to 12 hours, so checking in once or twice a day is enough. Battles are calculated server-side, so changing your device clock does nothing.

Is $HMANCER an investment?

No. It is a game token: its price is set by traders and can go to zero. Registration buys game entry and a Pre-Registration Box, not an investment, and there is no guarantee of any price, earnings, or return.

What happens when the 10,000 boxes sell out?

Pre-registration stays open at the same $1 — but it then buys game access only, no box. Your account is registered to the wallet that paid, you still receive the free ★1 starter hero at launch, and you summon more heroes with $HMANCER once summons open.

Can I play without an NFT hero?

Yes. Every registered account receives a free account-bound ★1 starter hero (Bram the Squire) when the game launches — it fights and levels like any hero, it just isn't an NFT and can't be transferred or sold. On top of that you can summon NFT heroes with $HMANCER, or have a Hero NFT transferred to your wallet by another player.

When exactly does $HMANCER launch?

The token is created before registrations open — a standard Solana mint with its authority revoked, the supply held in one public treasury. Trading opens December 15, 2026, when the liquidity pool opens; before that day the token already sits in players' wallets from their boxes but cannot be traded, because no pool exists. The contract address is published on this page first, and nowhere else — anything trading under the name before that is not ours.

How do I earn $HMANCER while playing?

Your heroes earn Loot automatically while they battle — sell that Loot for $HMANCER at the live rate, which floats with the Rewards Pool. More Party Power, higher zones, loot-bonus gear and boss kills all raise how much you earn. A Top-500 season finish pays an extra prize in SOL.
