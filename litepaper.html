HEROMANCER

The idle RPG that fights while you sleep — a browser idle hero-battler on Solana, powered by the $HMANCER token and star-rated Hero NFTs.
Status: pre-launch. This document describes the game's design. Nothing in it is a promise of price, earnings, or return.

1. Introduction

Heromancer is an idle RPG. You are the Heromancer — a summoner. Your hero party battles on its own: while you work, while you sleep, while the app is closed. You check in once or twice a day, claim the loot your heroes won, sell it for $HMANCER, and spend $HMANCER on gear, skills, and new heroes that make your party stronger. Like a classic MMO RPG, your account has a level: battles earn EXP, and leveling up unlocks harder zones, extra hero slots, and richer loot. And every hero you own is an NFT with a star rating (★1–★5) — playable in your party — your key to the game, carrying no promised monetary value. No reflexes. No grinding. Just heroes fighting and numbers going up, on-chain.

2. Heroes — star-rated NFTs

Every hero in Heromancer is an NFT on Solana, living in the player's own wallet — playable in-game, and above all the player's key to the game:

  • Star rating ★1–★5 — a hero's rarity and power. More stars means higher base power and a higher loot rate. ★1 is common; ★5 is the rarest and strongest.
  • Playable in-game — heroes form your party: they battle 24/7, earn Loot and EXP, and grow with your account level. Party size grows as your account levels up.
  • Access key, no promised value — a hero NFT carries no promised monetary value; its purpose is unlocking the game and fighting in your party. Heroes are transferable and the game reads your party from your wallet, so access follows the NFT — but nobody promises it can be sold for anything.
  • Star-up fusion — fuse duplicate heroes to raise a hero's star. Fused heroes are burned, so the hero supply shrinks as players build their ★5s.

Getting in: entry is a one-time $1 registration, paid in SOL. Every registration includes one Pre-Registration Box (Section 2A) that opens immediately: almost every box holds $HMANCER, and 20 of the 10,000 boxes hold the Genesis Warden — a ★★★ hero NFT, the rarest registration prize. There are exactly 10,000 boxes. Once they sell out, registration stays open at the same $1 — but it then buys game access only, no box: your account is registered to the wallet that paid, and you summon your first heroes with $HMANCER once summons open. One account per wallet.

3. The game loop

  1. Battle — your heroes auto-battle waves of monsters automatically, 24/7, earning Loot and EXP at your party's power rate.
  2. Claim — open the game and claim the accumulated loot. Offline earnings are capped at 12 hours, so checking in twice a day is optimal — never mandatory.
  3. Sell — sell Loot for $HMANCER in the in-game Market. The Loot→$HMANCER rate is set by the Rewards Pool (Section 5), not fixed, so rewards stay sustainable.
  4. Upgrade — spend $HMANCER on hero gear, skills, and hero levels. Every upgrade raises your party's power. A portion of every upgrade spend is burned.
  5. Summon — spend $HMANCER to summon new heroes (★1–★5; the odds are published in-game before summons open). Every summoned hero is an NFT you own — play it, fuse it, or trade it.
  6. Level up — battles earn EXP and your account level rises, MMO-style: higher account levels unlock harder zones, extra hero slots, and higher loot rates.
  7. Compete — a leaderboard ranks every account by season score (loot earned + power gained during the season). Seasons are monthly: the leaderboard resets on the 1st of every month and the finished season's Top 500 get paid. Seasons reset the race, not your heroes — heroes, gear, and account level are never reset.

Season 1 opens January 1, 2027 and runs to January 31; every season after that follows the calendar month.

5. The economy — sources and sinks

The economy is designed as a loop, not a faucet.

Sources of $HMANCER rewards. The Rewards Pool pays for loot. It is funded by 50% of every $1 registration and by trading fees from $HMANCER trading, from the trading launch on December 15, 2026. The Pre-Registration Box token prizes (50,000,000 $HMANCER, Section 2A) are paid from the game-rewards allocation, straight from the treasury. The Loot→$HMANCER rate floats with the pool's balance: a full pool pays generously, a thin pool pays less. Rewards can never exceed what the pool actually holds. Season rewards: the monthly Top 500 prizes (10,000 USDT in SOL, Section 3) are also paid from this pool after each season closes on the 1st of the month — loot payouts and season prizes share one pool and one rule: the pool only pays what it holds.

Sinks. Hero upgrades and summons (90% recycled to the Rewards Pool, 10% burned). Hero fusion burns duplicate hero NFTs. Future: season passes, cosmetics.

Project revenue. 50% of registration fees, plus a share of trading fees allocated to operations.

This structure means player rewards come from new players and trading activity — stated plainly, the way it should be.

6. Fair play

  • Battles are computed server-side; client tampering has no effect.
  • One account per wallet; hero ownership is read from the player's wallet.
  • Summon odds, the Rewards Pool balance, and the Loot→$HMANCER rate are displayed in-game, in the open.

Registration buys game entry and a Pre-Registration Box — not an investment. There is no guarantee of any price, earnings, or return.
